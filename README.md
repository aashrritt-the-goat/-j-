# Pronouncer's Desk

A spelling bee practice program. It reads each word aloud, lets you ask for clues the way you would at a real bee, and checks your typed spelling.

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. It uses the browser's built-in speech, so nothing needs to be installed.

## Word lists

- **Junior high study words (121):** words at the San Joaquin County (SJCOE) grades 7–9 level. This is a practice set, not the official SJCOE list.
- **Tricky words (25):** commonly misspelled words and past Scripps National Spelling Bee winning words.
- **My imported list:** paste the official SJCOE list (from your school or https://www.sjcoe.org/spellingbee/), one word per line. You can add clues on the same line, separated by `|`:
  `word | part of speech | definition | sentence | language of origin`

## How a round works

1. Press **Start the bee**. The pronouncer says your word twice.
2. Ask for **Repeat**, **Say it slowly**, **Definition**, **Use it in a sentence**, **Part of speech** or **Language of origin**. The word itself is never shown.
3. Type your spelling and press **Enter**. If you miss, it spells the word out letter by letter.
4. Missed words collect under **Words to review**. Use **Drill only these words** to practice them again.

Choose **Bee rules** to end the round on the first miss, like a real bee.
